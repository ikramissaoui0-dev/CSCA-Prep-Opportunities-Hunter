## One-page summary

**Coordinate geometry.** $|AB| = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$; midpoint $\left(\tfrac{x_1 + x_2}2, \tfrac{y_1 + y_2}2\right)$; slope $m = \tfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$. Parallel: $m_1 = m_2$; perpendicular: $m_1m_2 = -1$ (or $A_1A_2 + B_1B_2 = 0$). Point–line distance $\dfrac{|Ax_0 + By_0 + C|}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2}}$.

**Circles.** $(x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 = r^2$; general $x^2 + y^2 + Dx + Ey + F = 0$, centre $\left(-\tfrac D2, -\tfrac E2\right)$, $r = \tfrac12\sqrt{D^2 + E^2 - 4F}$. Chord $2\sqrt{r^2 - d^2}$; tangent iff $d = r$.

**Trigonometry.**

$$
\sin^2x + \cos^2x = 1, \quad \sin(\alpha \pm \beta) = \sin\alpha\cos\beta \pm \cos\alpha\sin\beta, \quad \cos(\alpha \pm \beta) = \cos\alpha\cos\beta \mp \sin\alpha\sin\beta, \quad \sin2x = 2\sin x\cos x, \quad \cos2x = 2\cos^2x - 1 = 1 - 2\sin^2x.
$$

Law of sines $\dfrac a{\sin A} = 2R$; law of cosines $a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc\cos A$; area $\tfrac12ab\sin C$.

**Conics.**

| | Equation | Relation | $e$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parabola | $y^2 = 2px$ (focus $(\tfrac p2, 0)$) | $\lvert PF\rvert = x_0 + \tfrac p2$ | $1$ |
| Ellipse | $\tfrac{x^2}{a^2} + \tfrac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$ | $c^2 = a^2 - b^2$ | $\tfrac ca < 1$ |
| Hyperbola | $\tfrac{x^2}{a^2} - \tfrac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$ | $c^2 = a^2 + b^2$ | $\tfrac ca > 1$ |

Asymptotes $y = \pm\tfrac bax$. Chord length $\sqrt{1 + k^2}\,|x_1 - x_2|$.

**Vectors.** $\vec a\cdot\vec b = |\vec a||\vec b|\cos\theta = x_1x_2 + y_1y_2$; perpendicular iff $0$; parallel iff $x_1y_2 = x_2y_1$.

**Complex numbers.** $i^2 = -1$; $z\bar z = |z|^2$; $z = r(\cos\theta + i\sin\theta)$; $z^n = r^n(\cos n\theta + i\sin n\theta)$.

**Solids.** $V_{\text{prism}} = Bh$; $V_{\text{pyramid/cone}} = \tfrac13Bh$; sphere $V = \tfrac43\pi R^3$, $S = 4\pi R^2$; space diagonal $\sqrt{a^2 + b^2 + c^2}$; distance to a plane $\dfrac{|Ax_0 + By_0 + Cz_0 + D|}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2 + C^2}}$.

## Common slips

- Mixing $c^2 = a^2 \mp b^2$ for ellipse and hyperbola.
- Forgetting a second solution (SSA) or a vertical line.
- Using degrees in radian formulas.

## Quick check

1. Centre and radius of $x^2 + y^2 - 2x + 4y - 4 = 0$.
2. $\vec a = (1, 2)$, $\vec b = (2, -1)$: perpendicular?
3. $(1 + i)^2 = ?$

**Answers.** (1) $(1, -2)$, $r = 3$. (2) $2 - 2 = 0$: yes. (3) $2i$.
