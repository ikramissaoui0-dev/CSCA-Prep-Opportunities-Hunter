## What you will learn

- the question types and a decision tree for choosing a tool;
- time and drawing tips; common traps.

## 1. Question types

| Type | Tool |
|---|---|
| Distances, midpoints, lines | coordinate formulas, general form of a line |
| Circle problems | centre and radius; compare $d$ with $r$ |
| Triangle problems | sine law (angle–opposite pairs), cosine law (SAS/SSS) |
| Trigonometric evaluation/simplification | identities, reference angles |
| Conics | $a, b, c, e$ and focal definition; substitute a line and use Vieta |
| Vectors | components, dot product, perpendicular/parallel tests |
| Complex numbers | $z = x + yi$, modulus and argument, de Moivre |
| Solid geometry | formulas for $V$ and $S$; coordinates and normals |

## 2. Decision tree: which tool?

- *Given two sides and the included angle, or three sides* $\Rightarrow$ law of cosines.
- *Given a side and its opposite angle, or two angles* $\Rightarrow$ law of sines.
- *Perpendicularity or an angle between lines/vectors* $\Rightarrow$ dot product.
- *A point and a line (distance)* $\Rightarrow$ point–line formula.
- *Line and conic intersection* $\Rightarrow$ substitute, discriminant, Vieta.
- *3D lengths and angles* $\Rightarrow$ coordinates.

## 3. Top tips

1. **Draw a diagram**, even a rough one; mark every given.
2. For circles, compare the distance from the centre with the radius.
3. For conics, write the standard form first; know which axis the foci lie on.
4. For vectors, convert geometry into dot products.
5. **Work the steps on paper**: do not skip algebra in your head.
6. If there is no diagram, sketch one from the description.
7. Check the answer: lengths positive, angles in range, special cases ($\Delta = 0$, vertical line).

## 4. Traps

- Missing the vertical tangent or vertical line.
- Two solutions in the SSA case.
- $c^2 = a^2 - b^2$ vs $a^2 + b^2$.
- Not taking absolute values for angles between lines or planes.

## Quick self-test

1. Distance from the origin to the line $3x + 4y - 10 = 0$.
2. An ellipse has $a = 5$ and $e = 0.6$. Find $b$.
3. In $\triangle ABC$: $a = 5$, $b = 7$, $c = 8$. Find $B$.

**Answers.** (1) $2$. (2) $c = 3$, $b = 4$. (3) $\cos B = \dfrac{25 + 64 - 49}{80} = \dfrac12$, $B = 60^\circ$.
