## What you will learn

- the question types and time allocation;
- strategies for limits, derivatives, integrals, probability and statistics.

## 1. Question types and time

| Type | Approach |
|---|---|
| Limits and continuity | substitute; if $\tfrac00$ factor/rationalise; at infinity divide by the highest power |
| Derivatives and tangents | identify the outermost operation (chain rule first) |
| Extrema, monotonicity, parameters | $f' = 0$, sign table, **verify the sign change** |
| Optimisation | variable, domain, critical points and endpoints |
| Integrals and areas | antiderivative, sketch, split where curves cross |
| Counting | ordered/unordered, with/without repetition; complement for "at least" |
| Probability | tree for conditionals; check independence before multiplying |
| Statistics | centre + spread; effect of $y = ax + b$ |

Aim for about $75$ seconds per question; skip and return if one takes over two minutes.

## 2. Top strategies

1. **Write every step**; partial credit and fewer slips.
2. **Check derivatives by integrating back**, and integrals by differentiating.
3. For optimisation, always state the allowed interval and compare endpoint values.
4. For probability, decide first whether the draws are with or without replacement.
5. For statistics, remember that variance changes by $a^2$ and the mean by $a\bar x + b$.
6. In "for all $x$" problems, isolate the parameter and use the max/min of a function.

## 3. Traps

- Forgetting the chain-rule factor.
- Using $f'(x_0) = 0$ as proof of an extremum.
- Forgetting $+C$ (indefinite) or including it (definite).
- Mixing up derivative and integral of $\sin$ and $\cos$.

## Quick self-test

1. $\lim_{x \to 0}\dfrac{e^{2x} - 1}x$.
2. Local extrema of $x^3 - 3x + 1$.
3. Ways to seat $4$ people chosen from $6$ in a row.

**Answers.** (1) $2$. (2) Local max $f(-1) = 3$; local min $f(1) = -1$. (3) $P(6, 4) = 360$.
