## One-page summary

**Functions basics.** Domain: largest set where the formula is defined. Even: $f(-x) = f(x)$; odd: $f(-x) = -f(x)$. Injective $\Rightarrow$ has an inverse.

**Inverse and composition.** $f^{-1}(f(x)) = x$; graphs symmetric in $y = x$; domain of $f^{-1}$ = range of $f$. $(f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x))$.

**Exponentials and logarithms.**

$$
a^ma^n = a^{m+n}, \quad (a^m)^n = a^{mn}, \quad a^{-n} = \frac1{a^n}, \quad \log_a(MN) = \log_aM + \log_aN, \quad \log_a\frac MN = \log_aM - \log_aN, \quad \log_aM^k = k\log_aM, \quad \log_ab = \frac{\log_cb}{\log_ca}.
$$

Growth: $A = A_0(1 + r)^t$; continuous $A = A_0e^{kt}$; half-life $A_0\left(\tfrac12\right)^{t/T}$.

**Transformations.** $f(x - h) + k$: right $h$, up $k$; $-f(x)$: flip in the $x$-axis; $f(-x)$: flip in the $y$-axis; $af(x)$: vertical stretch; $f(bx)$: horizontal compression by $b$.

**Sequences.** Arithmetic: $a_n = a_1 + (n - 1)d$, $S_n = \dfrac{n(a_1 + a_n)}2$, $2b = a + c$. Geometric: $a_n = a_1q^{n-1}$, $S_n = \dfrac{a_1(1 - q^n)}{1 - q}$, $S_\infty = \dfrac{a_1}{1 - q}$ for $|q| < 1$, $b^2 = ac$. $a_n = S_n - S_{n-1}$.

**Trigonometry.**

| $\theta$ | $0$ | $\dfrac\pi6$ | $\dfrac\pi4$ | $\dfrac\pi3$ | $\dfrac\pi2$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $\sin$ | $0$ | $\dfrac12$ | $\dfrac{\sqrt2}2$ | $\dfrac{\sqrt3}2$ | $1$ |
| $\cos$ | $1$ | $\dfrac{\sqrt3}2$ | $\dfrac{\sqrt2}2$ | $\dfrac12$ | $0$ |

$\pi\ \text{rad} = 180^\circ$; $s = r\theta$; $A = \tfrac12r^2\theta$. For $y = A\sin(\omega x + \varphi) + b$: period $\dfrac{2\pi}\omega$, range $[b - A, b + A]$. Inverse functions: $\arcsin \in [-\tfrac\pi2, \tfrac\pi2]$, $\arccos \in [0, \pi]$, $\arctan \in (-\tfrac\pi2, \tfrac\pi2)$.

## Common slips

- Reading $f^{-1}$ as $1/f$ and shifting graphs the wrong way.
- Adding exponents with different bases.
- Forgetting the log domain and the base-below-1 flip.
- Using degrees in a radian formula.

## Quick check

1. $\log_28 + \log_24 = ?$
2. Period of $y = 3\sin(4x)$.
3. $a_1 = 5$, $d = 2$: $a_{10}$?

**Answers.** (1) $5$. (2) $\dfrac\pi2$. (3) $23$.
