## What you will learn

- the mole, molar volume and the ideal gas equation;
- concentration, dilution and mass fraction conversions;
- limiting reagent and stoichiometry with gases and solutions.

## 1. Amount of substance

$$
n = \frac mM = \frac N{N_A} = \frac V{V_m} = cV, \qquad N_A = 6.02 \times 10^{23}\ \text{mol}^{-1}.
$$

**Molar volume** $V_m$: at STP ($0\ ^\circ\text{C}$, $101\ \text{kPa}$) $22.4\ \text{L/mol}$; at room temperature about $24\ \text{L/mol}$. **Avogadro's law**: equal volumes of gases at the same $T$ and $p$ contain the same number of molecules, so volume ratios equal mole ratios.

## 2. The ideal gas equation

$$
pV = nRT, \qquad R = 8.31\ \text{J/(mol K)} = 0.0821\ \text{L atm/(mol K)}.
$$

Use kelvin and consistent units. The density of a gas $\rho = \dfrac{pM}{RT}$.

**Example 1.** $0.50\ \text{mol}$ of gas at $300\ \text{K}$ and $2.0\ \text{atm}$: $V = \dfrac{0.5 \cdot 0.0821 \cdot 300}{2} = 6.2\ \text{L}$.

**Example 2.** At STP $8.4\ \text{L}$ of gas has $n = 0.375\ \text{mol}$; if its mass is $12\ \text{g}$, $M = 32\ \text{g/mol}$.

**Example 3.** Volume of $\text{H}_2$ (STP) from $4\ \text{g}$ of Mg with excess acid: $n = 0.167$, $V = 3.73\ \text{L}$.

## 3. Concentration and dilution

$c = \dfrac nV$ ($\text{mol/L}$, 物质的量浓度); dilution $c_1V_1 = c_2V_2$. **From mass fraction**: $c = \dfrac{1000\rho w}M$.

**Example 4.** Concentrated nitric acid ($63\%$, $\rho = 1.4\ \text{g/mL}$): $c = \dfrac{1000 \cdot 1.4 \cdot 0.63}{63} = 14\ \text{mol/L}$.

**Example 5.** To make $500\ \text{mL}$ of $2\ \text{mol/L}$ from $18\ \text{mol/L}$ $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$: $V_1 = \dfrac{500 \cdot 2}{18} = 55.6\ \text{mL}$ (add acid to water).

## 4. Gas stoichiometry

At the same $T$ and $p$ volumes are proportional to moles. $\text{CH}_4 + 2\text{O}_2 \to \text{CO}_2 + 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$: $10\ \text{mL}$ of methane needs $20\ \text{mL}$ of oxygen and gives $10\ \text{mL}$ of $\text{CO}_2$ (water is liquid after cooling, so the gas volume falls from $30$ to $10\ \text{mL}$).

**Limiting reagent with solutions**: $50\ \text{mL}$ of $2.0\ \text{mol/L}$ HCl ($0.100\ \text{mol}$) with $2.0\ \text{g}$ of Mg ($0.0823\ \text{mol}$, needing $0.165\ \text{mol}$ HCl): HCl is limiting, giving $0.050\ \text{mol}$ of $\text{H}_2$ ($1.12\ \text{L}$ at STP).

**Collecting gas over water**: $p_{\text{gas}} = p_{\text{total}} - p_{\text{H}_2\text{O}}$.

## Common mistakes

- Using $22.4\ \text{L}$ outside STP.
- Mixing $\text{mL}$ and $\text{L}$.
- Using Celsius in $pV = nRT$.

## Practice problems

1. Moles in $5.6\ \text{L}$ of gas at STP.
2. Volume of $0.2\ \text{mol}$ of gas at $300\ \text{K}$, $1\ \text{atm}$.

**Answers.** (1) $0.25\ \text{mol}$. (2) $4.9\ \text{L}$.
