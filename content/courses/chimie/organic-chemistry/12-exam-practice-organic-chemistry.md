## What you will learn

- the question types for organic chemistry;
- worked exam questions with answers;
- a reaction map to memorise.

## 1. Question types

| Type | Approach |
|---|---|
| Naming and drawing | longest chain, lowest locants |
| Homologous series | general formulae; trends in properties |
| Crude oil | fractional distillation; cracking |
| Reactions | functional group $\to$ reaction type, with reagents |
| Polymers | open the double bond; find the repeat unit |
| Isomers | systematic drawing; check duplicates |
| Pollution | sources, harm, remedies |

## 2. Reaction map

| From | To | Reagents and conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Alkane | Haloalkane | $\text{Cl}_2$ or $\text{Br}_2$, UV |
| Alkene | Alkane | $\text{H}_2$, Ni, heat |
| Alkene | Dihaloalkane | $\text{Br}_2$ (room temperature) |
| Alkene | Haloalkane | HBr (Markovnikov) |
| Alkene | Alcohol | steam, $\text{H}_3\text{PO}_4$, high pressure |
| Haloalkane | Alcohol | NaOH(aq), heat |
| Haloalkane | Alkene | NaOH in ethanol, heat |
| Alcohol | Alkene | conc. $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$, $170\ ^\circ\text{C}$ |
| 1° alcohol | aldehyde / acid | acidified $\text{K}_2\text{Cr}_2\text{O}_7$ (distil / reflux) |
| 2° alcohol | ketone | acidified $\text{K}_2\text{Cr}_2\text{O}_7$ |
| Acid + alcohol | ester + water | conc. $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$, reflux |

## 3. Worked questions

**Q1.** Draw all structural isomers of $\text{C}_3\text{H}_8\text{O}$ that are alcohols. *Answer*: propan-1-ol and propan-2-ol.

**Q2.** Ethene undergoes addition polymerisation: write the equation and name the polymer. *Answer*: $n\,\text{CH}_2{=}\text{CH}_2 \to (\text{–CH}_2\text{CH}_2\text{–})_n$, poly(ethene).

**Q3.** Give a two-step route from propene to propanone. *Answer*: hydrate propene with steam and acid to give propan-2-ol (Markovnikov), then oxidise it with acidified dichromate to propanone.

**Q4.** How many isomers has $\text{C}_4\text{H}_9\text{Br}$? *Answer*: four.

**Q5.** $0.1\ \text{mol}$ of an alkene gives $8.8\ \text{g}$ of $\text{CO}_2$ on burning: formula. *Answer*: $0.2\ \text{mol}$ $\text{CO}_2$, so $2$ carbons per molecule: $\text{C}_2\text{H}_4$.

**Q6.** Explain the environmental problems caused by burning fossil fuels in vehicles. *Answer*: $\text{CO}_2$ (global warming), $\text{CO}$ (toxic), $\text{NO}_x$ and $\text{SO}_2$ (acid rain, smog), soot and unburnt hydrocarbons.

**Q7.** Balance the cracking equation $\text{C}_{12}\text{H}_{26} \to \text{C}_2\text{H}_4 + ?$ *Answer*: $\text{C}_{10}\text{H}_{22}$.

**Q8.** Why does methylpropane boil lower than butane? *Answer*: its branched shape has less surface contact, so the London forces are weaker.

**Q9.** Distinguish propanal from propanone. *Answer*: warm with Tollens' reagent: a silver mirror for propanal only.

**Q10.** Name the ester from methanoic acid and ethanol. *Answer*: ethyl methanoate.

## 4. Quick reminders

General formulae: alkane $\text{C}_n\text{H}_{2n+2}$, alkene $\text{C}_n\text{H}_{2n}$, alcohol $\text{C}_n\text{H}_{2n+1}\text{OH}$. Addition has $100\%$ atom economy. Tertiary alcohols resist oxidation. Esters are named alcohol-yl acid-oate.

## Common mistakes

- Omitting conditions from a reaction.
- Confusing aqueous and ethanolic NaOH.
- Drawing the same isomer twice.

## Practice problem

How many structural isomers has $\text{C}_5\text{H}_{12}$?

**Answer.** Three.
