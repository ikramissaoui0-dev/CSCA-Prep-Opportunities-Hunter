## What you will learn

- the structure and properties of alcohols;
- how ethanol is made (fermentation and hydration);
- reactions of alcohols: combustion, oxidation, dehydration.

## 1. Structure and properties

Alcohols contain the **hydroxyl group** –OH: $\text{C}_n\text{H}_{2n+1}\text{OH}$ (methanol, ethanol, propanol). The –OH group forms **hydrogen bonds**, so alcohols have higher boiling points than alkanes of similar mass, and the small ones mix with water. Classification: **primary** ($\text{RCH}_2\text{OH}$), **secondary** ($\text{R}_2\text{CHOH}$), **tertiary** ($\text{R}_3\text{COH}$).

## 2. Making ethanol

| | Fermentation | Hydration of ethene |
|---|---|---|
| Reaction | $\text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}\text{O}_6 \to 2\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH} + 2\text{CO}_2$ | $\text{C}_2\text{H}_4 + \text{H}_2\text{O} \rightleftharpoons \text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH}$ |
| Conditions | yeast, about $35\ ^\circ\text{C}$, no air | steam, $\text{H}_3\text{PO}_4$, $300\ ^\circ\text{C}$, high pressure |
| Feedstock | renewable (sugar) | non-renewable (crude oil) |
| Rate | slow (batch) | fast (continuous) |
| Product | dilute, needs distillation | pure |

(With air, ethanol oxidises to vinegar.)

## 3. Reactions

- **Combustion**: $\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH} + 3\text{O}_2 \to 2\text{CO}_2 + 3\text{H}_2\text{O}$; $2\text{CH}_3\text{OH} + 3\text{O}_2 \to 2\text{CO}_2 + 4\text{H}_2\text{O}$ (methanol).
- **With sodium**: $2\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH} + 2\text{Na} \to 2\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{ONa} + \text{H}_2$ (slower than with water).
- **Oxidation** (acidified $\text{K}_2\text{Cr}_2\text{O}_7$, orange $\to$ green; or $\text{KMnO}_4$): primary alcohol $\to$ aldehyde $\to$ carboxylic acid (use reflux with excess oxidant for the acid, distil for the aldehyde); secondary alcohol $\to$ ketone; **tertiary alcohols resist oxidation**.
- **Dehydration** (conc. $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$, $170\ ^\circ\text{C}$): $\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH} \to \text{C}_2\text{H}_4 + \text{H}_2\text{O}$.
- **Esterification** with a carboxylic acid (next lesson).

**Example 1.** *Compare the two methods of making ethanol*: fermentation uses renewable sugar and mild conditions but is slow and gives an impure product; hydration is fast and gives pure ethanol but uses a non-renewable feedstock and high energy.

**Example 2.** Propan-1-ol oxidises to propanal then propanoic acid; propan-2-ol oxidises to propanone.

## 4. Uses

Fuels (bioethanol, methanol), solvents, drinks (ethanol), antiseptics, feedstock for esters and ethene.

## Common mistakes

- Thinking tertiary alcohols are oxidised by dichromate.
- Forgetting the conditions (reflux vs distillation).
- Saying alcohols are acidic like carboxylic acids.

## Practice problems

1. Equation for the combustion of methanol.
2. Oxidation product of butan-2-ol.

**Answers.** (1) $2\text{CH}_3\text{OH} + 3\text{O}_2 \to 2\text{CO}_2 + 4\text{H}_2\text{O}$. (2) Butanone.
