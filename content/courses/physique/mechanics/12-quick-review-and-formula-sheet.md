## Formula sheet

**Kinematics.** $v = v_0 + at$; $x = v_0t + \tfrac12at^2$; $v^2 = v_0^2 + 2ax$. Free fall $a = g$. Projectile: $R = \dfrac{v_0^2\sin2\theta}g$, $H = \dfrac{v_0^2\sin^2\theta}{2g}$, $T = \dfrac{2v_0\sin\theta}g$.

**Dynamics.** $\sum F = ma$; $W = mg$; $f_k = \mu_kN$, $f_s \le \mu_sN$; incline $a = g(\sin\theta - \mu\cos\theta)$; Atwood $a = \dfrac{(m_1 - m_2)g}{m_1 + m_2}$.

**Work, energy, power.** $W = Fd\cos\theta$; $E_k = \tfrac12mv^2$; $E_p = mgh$, $\tfrac12kx^2$; $W_{\text{net}} = \Delta E_k$; $P = Fv$; $v_{\max} = \dfrac Pf$.

**Momentum.** $p = mv$; $J = F\Delta t = \Delta p$; $m_1v_1 + m_2v_2 = m_1v_1' + m_2v_2'$; perfectly inelastic $v' = \dfrac{m_1v_1 + m_2v_2}{m_1 + m_2}$.

**Circular motion and gravity.** $v = \omega r = \dfrac{2\pi r}T$; $a_c = \dfrac{v^2}r = \omega^2r$; $F = \dfrac{GMm}{r^2}$; $GM = gR^2$; orbit $v = \sqrt{\dfrac{GM}r}$, $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{r^3}{GM}}$.

## Quick checks

1. A stone is dropped from rest: speed after $3\ \text{s}$ and distance fallen ($g = 10$).
2. A $1000\ \text{kg}$ car crosses a hump bridge of radius $40\ \text{m}$ at $10\ \text{m/s}$: normal force.
3. A $0.5\ \text{kg}$ ball hits a wall at $8\ \text{m/s}$ and rebounds at $6\ \text{m/s}$ in $0.02\ \text{s}$: average force.
4. Energy needed to lift $2\ \text{kg}$ by $3\ \text{m}$.

**Answers.** (1) $30\ \text{m/s}$, $45\ \text{m}$. (2) $10\,000 - 2500 = 7500\ \text{N}$. (3) $F = \dfrac{0.5 \cdot 14}{0.02} = 350\ \text{N}$. (4) $60\ \text{J}$.

## Common slips

- Forgetting the sign convention.
- Confusing mass and weight.
- Using conservation of energy when there is friction.
- Treating $F_c$ as an extra force.
