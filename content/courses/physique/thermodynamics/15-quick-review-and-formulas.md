## Formula sheet

**Heat.** $Q = mc\Delta T$; $Q = mL$; $\Delta L = \alpha L_0\Delta T$; conduction $\dfrac Qt = \dfrac{kA\Delta T}L$; radiation $P = \varepsilon\sigma AT^4$. $T(\text{K}) = t(^\circ\text{C}) + 273$.

**Gases.** $pV = nRT$; $\dfrac{p_1V_1}{T_1} = \dfrac{p_2V_2}{T_2}$; $\overline{E_k} = \tfrac32k_BT$; $v_{\text{rms}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{3RT}M}$; $U = \dfrac f2nRT$; $C_p = C_V + R$.

**First law.** $\Delta U = Q + W_{\text{on}} = Q - W_{\text{by}}$; isothermal $\Delta U = 0$; adiabatic $Q = 0$; isochoric $W = 0$; isobaric $W = p\Delta V$.

**Second law.** $\eta = \dfrac W{Q_H} = 1 - \dfrac{Q_C}{Q_H}$; $\eta_{\text{Carnot}} = 1 - \dfrac{T_C}{T_H}$; $\text{COP}_{\text{fridge}} = \dfrac{Q_C}W$; $\Delta S = \dfrac QT$; $\Delta S_{\text{isolated}} \ge 0$.

## Rapid check

1. $27\ ^\circ\text{C}$ in kelvin, and $\overline{E_k}$ at that temperature.
2. A gas absorbs $800\ \text{J}$ and $300\ \text{J}$ of work is done on it: $\Delta U$.
3. Heat to warm $2\ \text{kg}$ of water by $20\ \text{K}$.
4. Work at $10^5\ \text{Pa}$ from $1\ \text{L}$ to $3\ \text{L}$.

**Answers.** (1) $300\ \text{K}$; $6.21 \times 10^{-21}\ \text{J}$. (2) $+1100\ \text{J}$. (3) $167\ \text{kJ}$. (4) $200\ \text{J}$.

## Common slips

- Leaving temperatures in Celsius.
- Mixing up $C_V$ and $C_p$.
- Wrong sign of $W$.
