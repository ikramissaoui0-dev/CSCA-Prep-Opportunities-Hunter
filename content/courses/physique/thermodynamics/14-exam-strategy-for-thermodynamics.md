## What you will learn

- how to approach thermodynamics questions;
- worked strategy examples and the traps.

## 1. Strategy

1. **Identify the process** (isothermal, isobaric, isochoric, adiabatic) and write what is constant.
2. **Convert units immediately**: $^\circ\text{C} \to \text{K}$, $\text{L} \to \text{m}^3$, $\text{atm} \to \text{Pa}$, $\text{g} \to \text{kg}$.
3. **Write the governing equation** before substituting numbers.
4. **Fix a sign convention for $W$** and keep it.
5. **Sanity-check**: is the temperature rise reasonable? Is $\eta < \eta_{\text{Carnot}}$? Is the pressure positive?
6. For calorimetry, list **every heat flow** (warming, melting, warming again) and check which phase changes complete.
7. **Second-law** questions are usually yes/no: look for a violation (heat from cold to hot with no work, efficiency above Carnot).

In our reference mock papers, thermodynamics gets about $7$ of $48$ questions.

## 2. Worked strategy

*"An ideal gas in an insulated cylinder is quickly compressed by a piston."* Insulated and quick: adiabatic, $Q = 0$. Work is done on the gas, so $\Delta U > 0$ and $T$ rises; pressure rises more than for an isothermal compression.

*"$2\ \text{mol}$ of a monatomic gas is heated at constant volume from $300$ to $400\ \text{K}$."* $W = 0$; $Q = \Delta U = \tfrac32 \cdot 2 \cdot 8.31 \cdot 100 = 2494\ \text{J}$.

*"A heat engine absorbs $1000\ \text{J}$ at $600\ \text{K}$ and rejects $700\ \text{J}$ at $300\ \text{K}$."* $\eta = 0.3 < 0.5$: allowed.

## 3. Traps

- Rushing the conversions (leaving $^\circ\text{C}$).
- Getting the sign of $W$ wrong.
- Mixing up $C_V$ and $C_p$.
- Assuming an adiabatic process has no temperature change.
- Using $c_{\text{water}}$ for ice or steam.

## Quick self-test

1. Final temperature: $100\ \text{g}$ of water at $90\ ^\circ\text{C}$ and $50\ \text{g}$ at $30\ ^\circ\text{C}$.
2. Efficiency of a Carnot engine between $500\ \text{K}$ and $300\ \text{K}$.
3. Work done by a gas expanding from $2\ \text{L}$ to $5\ \text{L}$ at $2 \times 10^5\ \text{Pa}$.

**Answers.** (1) $70\ ^\circ\text{C}$. (2) $40\%$. (3) $600\ \text{J}$.
