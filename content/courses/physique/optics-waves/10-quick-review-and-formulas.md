## Formula sheet

**Waves.** $v = f\lambda$; $T = \dfrac1f$; string $v = \sqrt{T/\mu}$; Doppler $f' = f\dfrac{v \pm v_o}{v \mp v_s}$; $\beta = 10\log_{10}\dfrac I{I_0}$.

**Geometric optics.** $n = \dfrac cv$; $n_1\sin\theta_1 = n_2\sin\theta_2$; $\sin\theta_c = \dfrac{n_2}{n_1}$; $\lambda = \dfrac{\lambda_0}n$; mirror/lens $\dfrac1f = \dfrac1{d_i} + \dfrac1{d_o}$, $m = -\dfrac{d_i}{d_o}$; $f_{\text{mirror}} = \dfrac R2$; $P = \dfrac1f$.

**Wave optics.** Double slit $\Delta y = \dfrac{L\lambda}d$; single slit minima $a\sin\theta = m\lambda$; grating $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$; Malus $I = I_0\cos^2\theta$; thin film $2nt = (m + \tfrac12)\lambda$ (antireflection).

**Oscillations.** Spring $T = 2\pi\sqrt{m/k}$; pendulum $T = 2\pi\sqrt{L/g}$; $v_{\max} = A\omega$; $E = \tfrac12kA^2$. Standing waves: string/open pipe $f_n = \dfrac{nv}{2L}$; closed pipe $f = \dfrac{(2n - 1)v}{4L}$.

## Rapid check

1. Light from glass to air at $30^\circ$ ($n = 1.5$): refracted angle.
2. A $20\ \text{cm}$ converging lens with an object at $30\ \text{cm}$: image distance.
3. $\lambda$ in glass for $600\ \text{nm}$ light ($n = 1.5$).
4. Spring: $m = 0.4\ \text{kg}$, $k = 100\ \text{N/m}$: period.

**Answers.** (1) $\sin\theta = 0.75$, $\theta \approx 48.6^\circ$. (2) $60\ \text{cm}$. (3) $400\ \text{nm}$. (4) $0.40\ \text{s}$.

## Common slips

- Measuring angles from the surface.
- Frequency changing at an interface.
- Open/closed pipe patterns mixed up.
