## Formula sheet

**Electrostatics.** $F = \dfrac{kq_1q_2}{r^2}$; $E = \dfrac Fq = \dfrac{kQ}{r^2}$; uniform field $E = \dfrac Ud$; $\varphi = \dfrac{kQ}r$; $W_{AB} = qU_{AB}$; $C = \dfrac QU$; $E_C = \tfrac12CU^2$; capacitors: parallel $\sum C$, series $\dfrac1C = \sum\dfrac1{C_i}$.

**Circuits.** $U = IR$; $R = \dfrac{\rho L}A$; $P = UI = I^2R$; series $\sum R$; parallel $\dfrac1R = \sum\dfrac1{R_i}$; source $U = \varepsilon - Ir$, $I = \dfrac\varepsilon{R + r}$; Kirchhoff: $\sum I_{\text{in}} = \sum I_{\text{out}}$, $\sum\Delta U = 0$.

**Magnetism.** $B = \dfrac{\mu_0I}{2\pi r}$; $B = \mu_0nI$; $F = qvB\sin\theta$; $F = BIL\sin\theta$; $r = \dfrac{mv}{qB}$; $T = \dfrac{2\pi m}{qB}$; $v = \dfrac EB$.

**Induction and AC.** $\Phi = BA\cos\theta$; $\varepsilon = -N\dfrac{\Delta\Phi}{\Delta t}$; $\varepsilon = BLv$; $U_{\text{rms}} = \dfrac{U_0}{\sqrt2}$; $\dfrac{U_1}{U_2} = \dfrac{N_1}{N_2}$; $X_C = \dfrac1{\omega C}$, $X_L = \omega L$.

**Constants.** $k = 9 \times 10^9$; $e = 1.6 \times 10^{-19}\ \text{C}$; $\mu_0 = 4\pi \times 10^{-7}$.

## Rapid check

1. Two $6\ \Omega$ resistors in parallel in series with a $3\ \Omega$ across $12\ \text{V}$: current.
2. A transformer with $100$ primary turns, $500$ secondary and $20\ \text{V}$ in: secondary voltage and current ratio.
3. Charge on a $2\ \mu\text{F}$ capacitor at $50\ \text{V}$.

**Answers.** (1) $2\ \text{A}$. (2) $100\ \text{V}$; $I_1 : I_2 = 5 : 1$. (3) $100\ \mu\text{C}$.

## Common slips

- Adding parallel resistors directly.
- Using the resistor rule for capacitors.
- Adding electric fields as scalars.
- Assuming a step-up transformer raises power.
